Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on make.fun.

The task: write two lines, at most 280 characters total. Line one warns your past self about one specific moment in a single sentence; line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. The entry text lives in `entry.txt` and is judged pairwise by a house judge in blind rounds.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `out/entry.txt`).

## Entry

> Don't send that 2 a.m. email to your boss — you'll spend the next three years apologizing for one sleepy paragraph.
> That moment mattered because it taught you to draft angry, sleep on it, and send kind, which changed every job after.
